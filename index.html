Finding the cinematic

Hi, I'm Daniel. I'm a photographer based in Cologne, heavily focused on urban life, architecture, and brands across Europe and East Asia.

I’m a huge film enthusiast, which deeply shapes the way I see the world. When I wander through a city, I’m always looking for that unexpected angle—a new perspective on something familiar. For me, photography isn't just about documenting what a location looks like; it's about capturing how it actually feels to be there.

For the past 15 years, I’ve been an exclusive contributor to picture alliance (dpa, the German Press Agency), building an archive of more than 10,000 images. Whether I'm shooting a metropolis at night or a quiet architectural detail, my goal is always to tell the story of that space.
